Privacy policy

server-backup is used only by its owner. It asks Google for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it created itself in the owner's Google Drive.

It uploads encrypted backup files of the owner's own server and deletes old ones. It does not read any other Drive files, does not collect data about anyone else, and does not share data with anyone.

To revoke access, remove "server-backup" at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
